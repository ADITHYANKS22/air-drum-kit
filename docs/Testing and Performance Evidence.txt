3. QUALITATIVE TEST OBSERVATIONS & FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. False Positive Suppression (Hovering vs Striking):
   - Resting a hand or stick marker inside a drum pad's bounding box without
     downward motion generates zero audio events.
   - The downward velocity gate (vy >= 180 px/s) successfully prevents accidental
     triggers during idle positioning.

2. Double-Hit Mitigation:
   - Rapid oscillations, hand tremors, or micro-rebounds immediately following
     a strike are blocked by the 150 ms cooldown timer, eliminating acoustic
     machine-gun artifacts.

3. Tracking Jitter Reduction:
   - MediaPipe fingertip coordinates can show micro-jitter under low-light
     conditions. Applying an Exponential Moving Average (EMA, alpha = 0.1)
     produces smooth pad interaction without perceptual tracking delay.

4. Ambient Lighting Adaptability:
   - For colored stick-tracking mode, ambient fluorescent or warm lighting
     shifts HSV boundaries. Using the interactive calibration tool
     (scripts/demo_hsv_calibration.py) allows real-time tuning and direct JSON
     persistence to assets/layouts/hsv.json without code modification.

5. Simultaneous Polyphonic Playback:
   - Striking two pads simultaneously (e.g., Hi-Hat and Snare with both hands)
     triggers distinct simultaneous audio samples without channel clipping or
     audio stutter.
